Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Who operates the app

REGAL TAP RUSH is operated by David Vasilca under the REGAL GAMES brand. For support or privacy questions, email support.regalgames@gmail.com.

Information in the game

The version 1.0 App Store build has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, game-account service or REGAL GAMES game-data server. It does not send your gameplay, device identifiers or purchase history to REGAL GAMES. The Google advertising and consent SDKs used during development are not included in this release.

Game data stored on your device

Scores, XP, ranks, completed runs, currency balances, daily reward status, owned/selected cosmetics and sound/haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. The local wallet records credited purchase transaction and reward identifiers to prevent duplicate delivery. The app provides no cross-device balance sync or account-based progress recovery.

Removing the app removes its local progress and wallet. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your settings. Permanent Apple purchase entitlements can be restored through the App Store; spent or previously delivered consumable Coins are not restored. Do not delete the app expecting Restore Purchases to recover your Coin balance.

Apple in-app purchases

Optional purchases are Founder Gold, a permanent cosmetic, and consumable packs of 500 or 2,000 Coins. Prices are displayed in your App Store currency before purchase. There are no subscriptions. Remove Ads is unavailable for new purchases in this ad-free release; existing verified ownership can still be restored.

Product loading, receipt checks, purchases and restoration communicate with Apple App Store services over the network. Apple processes payment and account information under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game does not receive your payment-card number. StoreKit provides signed product and transaction information to deliver purchases, check permanent entitlements and prevent duplicate Coins. This information is verified and used on your device; REGAL GAMES does not send it to a separate server.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments to respond to your request. Our support address uses Gmail, so Google processes and stores that correspondence as the email provider. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you send to support. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule is promised in this notice. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests, including IP addresses, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. These pages contain no scripts, analytics tags, cookies, external fonts or embedded advertisements.

Audience and changes

The game serves a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Apple purchase controls apply to in-app purchases. This notice describes version 1.0; adding advertising, tracking, accounts or other data services would require a new review of privacy disclosures and any applicable consent requirements before activation.
