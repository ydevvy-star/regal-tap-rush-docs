Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Pre-release information. REGAL TAP RUSH is being prepared for its first App Store release. Production advertising and real-money purchases are currently disabled. Test integrations described here do not mean live advertising or payments are available.

Who operates the app

REGAL TAP RUSH is operated by David Vasilca under the REGAL GAMES brand. For support or privacy questions, email support.regalgames@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

The app saves your scores, XP, completed runs, coin balance and owned/selected cosmetics locally. The development wallet also records identifiers for credited purchase transactions and ad rewards to prevent duplicate delivery. The app has no game-account service, cross-device balance recovery or separate REGAL GAMES game-data server. Removing the app removes local data; Apple backups may retain app data according to your settings.

Advertising and consent integration

The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.11.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 3.1.0. Ad requests are currently limited to explicit development testing with Google's demo ad units. Production ad serving is disabled.

Google's documented SDK practices include processing IP addresses/general location, device or app identifiers, ad events and interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics for functions such as advertising and measurement. The exact collection, linkage and purposes for the final publisher configuration require verification. No claim is made that non-personalized ads collect no data or cannot involve tracking.

The test flow updates UMP consent information, presents required messages, checks whether the SDK permits ad requests and provides privacy options when required. Errors block requests in this implementation. The app currently does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization. Final regional messages, withdrawal behavior, any ATT requirement and App Store privacy disclosures must be verified before production activation.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's iOS SDK data-disclosure documentation for the provider's practices.

Apple purchases

StoreKit 2 is used for development purchase testing. Production payments are disabled. Apple processes payment/account information under Apple's Privacy Policy; the game does not receive your payment-card number. Verified product and transaction identifiers support delivery and restoration. Coin-credit identifiers are retained locally to prevent replay. Actual Apple-server Sandbox testing and final purchase-support behavior remain to be verified.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments to respond to your request. Our support address uses Gmail, so Google processes and stores that correspondence as the email provider. Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you send to support. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule is promised in this notice.

This website

The proposed public host is GitHub Pages. If publication is approved, GitHub will process website requests, including IP addresses, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. These pages contain no scripts, analytics tags, cookies, external fonts or embedded advertisements.

Audience and changes

The intended audience is general and the game is not specifically directed at children. This does not replace the final App Store age-rating assessment or applicable regional consent requirements. This notice will be reviewed when release features or verified data practices change.
