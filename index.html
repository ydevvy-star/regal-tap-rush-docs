REGAL TAP RUSH

Support

Help with playing, progress and privacy.

Pre-release information. REGAL TAP RUSH is being prepared for its first App Store release. Production advertising and real-money purchases are currently disabled. Test integrations described here do not mean live advertising or payments are available.

How to play

Tap glowing circles and gold stars, avoid pink crosses and build combos. A run ends when the 60-second timer expires or you lose all three lives. Use Pause when you need a break.

Progress and cosmetics

Runs earn coins and XP. Coins unlock cosmetic colours; colours do not change scoring or difficulty. Scores, rank, coins and selected cosmetics are saved locally on your device. No game account or cross-device sync is provided. Removing the app removes local progress; device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings.

Ads and purchases

Development includes optional rewarded ads, interstitials between rounds, Founder Gold cosmetics, coin packs and Remove Ads. These are not enabled for production. In the test integration, an earned rewarded-ad callback grants 25 coins. Automatic interstitials are limited to a new-round transition after every third completed round, with at least 120 seconds between them. Remove Ads suppresses automatic interstitials; rewarded ads remain voluntary.

Local purchase simulations have been tested. Apple-server Sandbox purchases are not yet verified. Permanent cosmetic and Remove Ads entitlements are designed to restore; consumed coin packs do not restore. No subscription is planned in the current product design. Live product availability and prices must be confirmed before release.

Privacy choices

The development integration uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When required, the app provides an Ad privacy choices entry in Privacy & Help. Publisher consent configuration and regional behavior are still being verified. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Email support.regalgames@gmail.com. Include your app version, iPhone/iOS version and a description of the problem. Never send passwords, verification codes, bank information or payment-card details. Avoid including sensitive information in screenshots.
