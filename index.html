Support

Help with REGAL TAP RUSH version 1.0.

How to play

Tap glowing circles and gold stars, avoid pink crosses and build combos. A run ends when the 60-second timer expires or you lose all three lives. Use Pause when you need a break.

Coins and cosmetics

Qualifying completed rounds earn 5 Coins, with +5 for good performance and +10 more for excellent performance. The in-game reward guide explains the score and active-play requirements. Return daily for 20 Coins. Coins unlock cosmetic themes; rank unlocks reward longer-term play. Cosmetics never change scoring or difficulty.

Optional purchases

Founder Gold is a one-time permanent cosmetic purchase. The 500 Coins and 2,000 Coins packs are consumable purchases for the cosmetic shop. The shop displays Apple's current localized price; review Apple's confirmation sheet before buying. There are no subscriptions and no advertisements in this release.

Remove Ads is temporarily unavailable for new purchases because this release contains no ads. Existing verified Remove Ads ownership is preserved and can be restored.

Restore and purchase help

Open Shop → Premium → Restore Purchases to recover permanent purchases using the Apple Account that bought them. Restore does not deliver another copy of a previously consumed Coin pack and does not recover spent Coins or a deleted local balance. For an unfinished purchase, reopen the game with network access so StoreKit can retry verified delivery. Pending purchases grant nothing until approved. If a purchase is cancelled, nothing is unlocked.

For billing or refund help, use Apple's purchase support. For a delivery problem, email us with your app/iOS version and the product name; never send payment-card details, passwords or verification codes.

Saved progress

Progress and wallet balances are saved on this device. No game account or cross-device sync is provided. Removing the app removes local progress; Apple backups may include app data according to your settings. Do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot a Coin balance.

Privacy and contact

Read our Privacy Policy. Contact support.regalgames@gmail.com with a description of the problem and your app/iPhone/iOS version. Avoid sensitive information in screenshots. REGAL GAMES is operated by David Vasilca.
